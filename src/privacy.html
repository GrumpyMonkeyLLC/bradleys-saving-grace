Your Information

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Bradley's Hugs is a nonprofit that helps reunite lost and stolen dogs with their families. This page explains what information we collect through this website, what we do with it, and how you can ask us to change or remove it. We keep it simple: we only collect what we need to help find dogs and stay in touch, and we never sell your information.

What we collect

Lost and stolen dog reports

When you report a dog, we collect:

  • Your dog's details: name, breed, color, size, age, gender, microchip information, description, and photo
  • Where and when your dog went missing
  • Your name, phone number, and email address

Contact and partnership messages

When you send us a message, we collect your name, email address, and your message. Your phone number and organization details are optional.

Email updates

If you sign up for updates, we collect your email address.

Donations

Donations made through Venmo, Cash App, or Zeffy are handled entirely by those services. We don't see or store your card or bank details. Each one has its own privacy policy. If you mail a check, we use your name and address only to process your gift and send a receipt if you ask for one.

What we make public

Lost dog reports only work if people can see them. When you submit a report, it is posted publicly on our Lost Dogs page so anyone who spots your dog can reach you.

This includes your name, phone number, and email address, along with your dog's details, photo, and last-seen location. If you choose to create a printable flyer, it includes the same information and can be viewed by anyone with the link. You agree to this when you check the consent box on the report form.

Contact messages, partnership inquiries, and email sign-ups are never made public. Only Bradley's Hugs staff can see them.

How we use your information

  • To post and share lost dog reports so dogs can be found
  • To make printable flyers when you ask for one
  • To reply to your messages and partnership inquiries
  • To send updates you signed up for
  • To keep the website running and protect it from misuse

We don't sell, rent, or trade your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who else handles your information

We rely on a few services to run this website:

  • Microsoft Azure hosts the website and stores reports, photos, flyers, and messages.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts on this site. Loading them shares your IP address with Google.
  • Venmo, Cash App, and Zeffy process donations and event tickets when you use them.

We may also share information with law enforcement or animal control when it helps recover a stolen dog, or when the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising cookies, analytics, or tracking pixels. The only cookie this site sets is a sign-in cookie for our staff when they log in to manage reports.

How long we keep it

Lost dog reports stay on the site while the search is active. When a dog is found, we mark the report as reunited and may keep it to share the good news. We'll take any report down when you ask. We keep messages as long as we need them to respond and follow up, and email addresses until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Update or correct your report
  • Remove your report, photo, or flyer from the site
  • Delete a message you sent us
  • Stop sending you email updates

Email us at bradleyssavinggrace@gmail.com and we'll take care of it as soon as we can.

Children

This website isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
